# LifeSTONE companion app

Game log and statistics for the LifeSTONE life counter for Magic: The Gathering
(Waveshare RP2350 Touch AMOLED 1.8).

**Open it:** https://sergiodesa.github.io/mtgstone-app/

- iPhone: Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*
- Android: Chrome → *Install app*

Scan the history QR code on the device (BOOT → History → Export QR) to keep a log of your
games with life chart, commander damage and history, and see win rates per player and deck.
Everything stays on your phone and works offline.

This repository is published automatically; please don't edit it by hand.

Fonts: Montserrat and Boldonse (SIL Open Font License, see `fonts/`).
QR decoding: jsQR (Apache 2.0, see `vendor/`). Card names: Scryfall.
